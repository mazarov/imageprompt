Скопируйте промпт ниже как **единый блок**:

```txt
Chrome Web Store small promotional banner EXACT SIZE 440x280 px, PNG, crisp edges, readable at thumbnail scale.

Creative direction ("Lite" edition): fast + minimal + airy; high contrast versus a white listings page background.

BACKGROUND:
- Full-bleed smooth diagonal gradient: cyan/teal (#0891b2) -> indigo (#4f46e5) -> purple (#7e22ce).

COMPOSITION:
- Center a large white rounded "product card".
- LEFT: minimalist photo thumbnail (dark frame, tiny sun + green mountains silhouette).
- CENTER: bold white/right-arrow chevron implying transformation.
- RIGHT: bright cyan rounded speech bubble with 3 horizontal "text lines" (abstract, no readable words besides the headline).

ACCENTS:
- Small yellow sparkle stars near bubble (implies AI magic), but subtle.
- Top-right translucent pill badge with word: LITE (white letters on glassy capsule).

TEXT (minimal, English; keep short because non-native users):
- Bottom headline in huge bold white sans: IMAGE → PROMPT
- Smaller muted line beneath: AI IMAGE DESCRIBER

Do NOT add watermark, logos, fake Chrome/Web Store badges, screenshots, noisy textures, QR codes, crowded paragraphs, lens flares, or tiny unreadable captions.
Avoid white-only backgrounds; avoid cluttered competitor-style diagrams.

```

После генерации проверьте: **PNG ≤ 800 KB**, размер строго **440×280**.
